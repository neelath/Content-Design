RevFlo System Introduction

What is RevFlo?

RevFlo is a Salesforce-integrated application that enables sales teams to speed up sales cycles by negotiating and executing prices, quotations, and contracts with AI-driven price guidance and margin analysis.

Price Guidance Methodology

Price Index Formula

PI = Actual Price / (COGS + SSE)

Where: PI = Price Index, COGS = Cost of Goods Sold, SSE = Sales & Selling Expenses

Pricing Use Cases

1. General Renewal

When to Use:

Existing products for existing customers with purchase history

Pricing Logic:

  • Target price anchored to customer's previous price index
  • Drift adjustment based on segment variability
  • Low-performing customers get larger increments
  • High-performing customers get smaller increases

2. Renewal with Additional Products

When to Use:

New products for existing customers (no purchase history for these products)

Pricing Logic:

  • No self-reference price available
  • Target based on segment-level recommendation
  • Uses weighted average price index plus segment drift

3. New Customer Agreement

When to Use:

New customers with no purchase history from Solenis

Pricing Logic:

  • Default to predefined attribute values
  • Option to borrow attributes from sponsor customer
  • Segment-based pricing recommendations
Important Decision Required: For new customers, determine who assigns sponsor customers and where this assignment occurs (RevFlo or Price Guidance system).

Best Practices & Tips

Quote Creation Best Practices

💡 Preparation

  • Gather customer requirements before starting
  • Review existing agreements and performance
  • Check for any pending price changes
  • Verify customer master data accuracy

🎯 Price Guidance Usage

  • Always review peer reports before pricing
  • Understand the segment logic behind recommendations
  • Document deviations with business justification
  • Use compliance indicators as guidelines

✅ Quality Checks

  • Verify all required fields are completed
  • Double-check quantities and units of measure
  • Confirm distributor margins for indirect sales
  • Review total quote value and margins

🚀 Efficiency Tips

  • Use Excel templates for multi-product quotes
  • Save frequently used product combinations
  • Leverage quote cloning for similar customers
  • Set up email notifications for approvals

Common Pitfalls to Avoid

  • Ignoring Price Guidance: Always review recommendations even if you plan to deviate
  • Incomplete Documentation: Poor justification leads to approval delays
  • Wrong Quote Type: Selecting incorrect direct/indirect classification
  • Missing Approvals: Not accounting for all required approval levels
  • Data Quality Issues: Using outdated customer or product information

Performance Optimization

  • Speed: Target quote creation in under 30 minutes for standard deals
  • Accuracy: Aim for first-time approval rate above 90%
  • Compliance: Stay within price guidance 80%+ of the time
  • Customer Service: Provide quotes within 24-48 hours of request

Q&A Session Preparation

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What happens to my existing quotes in the old system?

A: Existing quotes will be migrated during the transition period. Active agreements will be converted to the new system format with full history preservation.

Q: How accurate is the price guidance?

A: Price guidance is based on historical data and statistical models. Accuracy improves over time as more data is collected. Typical accuracy rates are 85-90% for established segments.

Q: Can I override price guidance recommendations?

A: Yes, with proper justification and approval. The system is designed to guide, not restrict. Business judgment remains critical for complex situations.

Q: What if my customer isn't in the system?

A: You can create quotes for prospects. Once the customer is approved, a SAP customer number will be created and the quote can be finalized.

Q: How long does the approval process take?

A: Typical approval times range from 4-24 hours depending on complexity and approval levels required. Emergency processes are available for urgent situations.

Technical Questions

Q: What browsers are supported?

A: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge (latest versions). Internet Explorer is not supported.

Q: Can I work offline?

A: Limited offline capability is available for viewing existing data. Quote creation and price guidance require internet connectivity.

Business Process Questions

Q: How do I handle special pricing situations?

A: Document special circumstances in quote notes and work with your pricing analyst for guidance. Emergency approval processes are available.
